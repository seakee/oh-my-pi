`proc://` lists jobs and services, and `proc://<id>` shows one process's status or output. Writing sends stdin, where empty content is a bare Enter, and `proc://<id>/kill` cancels or stops the process with no `content`. `proc://<id>/mode` takes `persist`, `session`, or `detached`. `proc://<id>/streams` lists a bash job's pipes, and `proc://<id>/streams/<n>` reads what crossed pipe `n` so far, staying live under `| tail` or `| grep`; never rerun the command just to see it.
