`proc://` lists jobs, agents, and services, and `proc://<id>` shows status or output. Writing to `proc://<id>/kill` with empty `content` cancels or stops jobs, agents, and services. `proc://<id>/streams` reads a bash job's pipes so far (live under `| tail` or `| grep`; never rerun a command just to see it). Parked agents or a missing job entry do not mean lost output; check `agent://<id>` or `history://<id>`.
